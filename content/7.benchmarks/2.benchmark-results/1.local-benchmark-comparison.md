## Local backend performance (comparison)

Results from `make stress-local-compare`.

All runs used:

- `--provider local --threads 16 --duration-s 30 --window-s 10`
- `sample_every=100`

Note: `make stress-local-compare` runs both `absolute` and `suppressed` strategy variants for `trypema`.

## Results (Stress / Load harness)

These results are copied from a `make stress` run (the `trypema-stress` harness). Units:

- Throughput: `ops_per_s` (ops/sec)
- Latency: `lat_us` in microseconds (sampled every `sample_every=100` ops)

`key_dist` controls how the stress test chooses rate-limiter keys (users) on each operation:

- `hot`: always uses a single key (`user_0`). `key_space` is ignored (effectively 1 key).
- `uniform`: picks a random key uniformly from `key_space` keys (`user_0..user_{key_space-1}`).
- `skewed`: with probability `hot_fraction` uses `user_0`, otherwise picks uniformly from the remaining keys (`user_1..`). (`key_space` controls how many exist.)

| Target | Provider | Strategy | Mode | Threads | Duration (s) | Key dist | Key space | Offered load | ops/s | p50 (us) | p95 (us) | p99 (us) | p99.9 (us) | max (us) | allowed | rejected | suppressed_denied | errors |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `make stress-local-hot` | local | absolute | max | 16 | 30 | hot | 100000 | max | 3,581,224 | 1 | 5 | 30 | 107 | 24,239 | 30,014 | 107,419,455 | 0 | 0 |
| `make stress-local-uniform` | local | absolute | max | 16 | 60 | uniform | 100000 | max | 8,354,304 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 145 | 1,470 | 501,590,138 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `make stress-local-burst` | local | suppressed | target-qps | 16 | 120 | skewed | 100000 | `target=20k, burst=200k` | 683,740 | 1 | 6 | 23 | 484 | 9,031 | 16,625,173 | 0 | 65,450,386 | 0 |
